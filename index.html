01 / OUR STORY

More than
a cricket team.

11 Friends is a local cricket family from Thanjavur. What started with friends playing together grew into a team that keeps choosing its own people, its own identity and its own way of playing the game.

Every match adds another memory. Every tournament adds another chapter. The jersey represents more than eleven players — it represents everyone who stands with the team.

“11 Friends. Enough.”
